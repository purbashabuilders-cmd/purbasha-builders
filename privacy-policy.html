Enquiry Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Purbasha Builders handles information submitted through our property enquiry forms, including forms on Facebook and Instagram.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, phone number, email address and any other information you choose to provide about your property requirements.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, share details about available properties, arrange site visits and follow up on your request. Our team may contact you by phone, email or message for these purposes.

Who may receive your information

Your information may be accessed by members of the Purbasha Builders team and service providers who help us manage enquiries and communications. Meta also processes information submitted through its platforms under its own privacy policy.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to and manage the enquiry, meet applicable legal requirements and resolve related issues. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction or deletion of your enquiry information, withdraw your consent where applicable, or ask us to stop contacting you. We will consider your request in accordance with applicable law.
