Before you visit

Before
your visit.

Can I see the actual floor plans?

Yes—request the latest approved drawings from the builder through the enquiry form. The drawings on this preview website are illustrative demos and do not represent approved project layouts.

How do I get the current price and availability?

Choose the home you are interested in and send an enquiry. The team will confirm the current price, availability, area definitions and applicable charges.

Can I visit while construction is underway?

Request a site visit and let the team confirm a suitable time, the meeting point and any site-access requirements. Please wait for confirmation before travelling.

What should I check before making a booking?

Ask for the project registration, approved drawings, agreement terms, payment schedule, possession commitments and area calculations. Review the current documents before deciding.
